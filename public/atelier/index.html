Enamel reserve is available for Vase. Broad reflective metal surrounds blue filled cells. Both materials lie flush on the original surface; the shape and cast shadow stay fixed. Pigment-only treatments keep the blue and silver colors without their reflections. Metal-and-enamel reference.

Shingle relief is available for Vase. Broad plate tips and short recess shadows create relief on a solid backing. The silhouette and cast shadow keep the original vase geometry. The inside remains plain, and pigment-only treatments show uniform gray. Overlapping-scale reference.

Perforated metal is available for Vase and Sphere. Its openings let the background and light through, without adding hole rims. Original-shape studies such as Section ribs and Thickness exposure keep their own geometry. Perforated metal reference.

Three.js renders materials, shadows, normals, depth and mesh identity. p5.js interprets that exact frame as ink, thread, dots or engraved lines. Each treatment changes the drawing mechanism rather than merely its palette.
