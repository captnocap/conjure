# Variable-scene Atelier geometry proxies

`public/atelier/conjure-proxies.js` adapts Section ribs, Single cutaway, and Interior lattice to an immutable Conjure geometry capture. The original Atelier Garden implementations, accepted bakes, signatures, and exact paths remain intact. Only a snapshot marked `source: 'conjure'` enters these adapters.

The adapters operate on transformed source triangles, including separate instances and deformation copied by the capture bridge. Material groups sharing a `bodyId` are joined for topology, while original faces retain their own material pigments. Positional welding joins authored hard-normal and UV seams before checking edge incidence. Negative-scale instances restore outward winding. Open or non-manifold bodies remain sheets; the adapters do not invent a closed interior for them.

## Mechanisms and adaptations

- **Section ribs:** Two world-vertical plane families intersect each source body along x and z. Closed contours are triangulated with nested holes intact, then extruded to finite gauge. Open surfaces produce finite-width, finite-gauge ribbons along their actual intersection curves. Plane count is bounded to eleven per axis, with pitch and gauge scaled to the body and owner extent. The ground stays intact. Unlike the original fixed Garden bake, all profiles are constructed from the current capture.
- **Single cutaway:** The original oblique plane normal `[0.36, 0, 1]` is retained. Its offset is placed 57% through the cast's projected extent (IDs 3 onward), or the non-ground scene extent when there is no cast. It clips every non-ground source triangle. Only closed bodies receive red section caps, with contour holes preserved. Open surfaces and alpha-cutout bodies have uncapped cut edges. Using the cast to place the common plane prevents a distant building from moving the cut entirely away from the subject.
- **Interior lattice:** A body-centred cubic grid proposes centre-to-corner bars. Three parity rays classify endpoints against real triangles; the original Atelier whole-segment/triangle distance predicate certifies clearance for the complete square prism, including endpoint extension. Predicate coordinates are normalized about each body's centre so that dimensional distance and dimensionless barycentric tolerances remain meaningful at very small and very large world scales; emitted vertices remain in their original world coordinates. A BVH prunes tests without replacing the solid with an occupancy box. Pitch, width and clearance scale with each body and owner. Open surfaces, ground and details below 5.5% of owner extent retain their original triangles, following the source study's original-surface hybrid. Some small closed regions can have no accepted bars, as in the original study.

All three build their own proxy depth and hard key-shadow raster. Removed solids cannot leave their old shadows behind. Camera projection, near/far clipping, directional-light colors and pigments come from the capture; there is no fixed-camera hash admission. A world without directional lights receives only the matte ambient term. Orthographic and perspective projections are supported. Source-style matte shading deliberately omits PBR reflections and transmission.

Section ribs retain the source contrast response; the other two constructions remain independent of seed and shared strength. Existing mixer IDs, layering, worker cancellation, retained plan ownership and cold/warm replay protocols are unchanged. Dynamic field models are discriminated from Garden fields and use 32-bit sample ownership rather than the Garden bake's fixed palette size. Cutaway preserves the established `Uint8Array` worker image contract.

## Limits

These are numerical rendering proxies, not certified CSG or fabrication meshes. Shader-only holes and texture alpha are not represented as geometry in the capture; alpha-cutout bodies therefore remain uncapped sheets and are not converted to a lattice. Their original triangle outlines can still appear solid in a proxy. Open or non-manifold components cannot be assigned volumetric caps or bars. Material textures, procedural relief, glass transmission and reflections remain outside these matte proxies, as they do in the source studies. Partial/overlay mixtures use the original image masks and can retain lower solid imagery outside the selected mask.

## Focused verification

`node --import tsx tests/atelier-proxies.test.ts` covers true closed sections and torus holes, uncapped open cylinders, a shared cut plane with real caps, multi-material body closure, whole-prism containment, scale invariance, dynamic palette validation, byte ownership, exact replay, cutaway cold/cached worker protocol, and contrast/camera changes. Browser and scene-switching evidence is recorded by the aggregate Atelier integration check.
