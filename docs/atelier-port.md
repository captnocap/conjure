## Shared runtime and capture dependencies

Every row uses `styles.js`, `mixer.js`, `contact-worker.js`, `drawing-protocol.js`, `render-source.js`, and the isolated vendored p5 1.x renderer. The browser dependency-graph test checks these imports, including worker-only files. Geometry is serialized through the worker snapshot, not only attached to a main-thread frame.

The native frame supplies captured color, normal, linear depth, owner IDs, diffuse light, material metadata and source-owned preparation caches. `engine/atelier-capture.ts` preserves draw ranges, groups, stable owners/body identities, instance transforms, current deformation, material sidedness, lights and actual camera matrices. Explicit ground metadata replaces array-position assumptions. Per-pixel metalness, true linear albedo and transmission are captured and passed through the worker. Native mapped pigments retain their UV channels, vertex colors and alpha handling; the 22 Atelier materials provide their original procedural pigment companions. Transmission retains the source factor and red-channel transmission map. `f.B(x,y)` reads the captured pigment, converted from linear RGB to display RGB, and `f.T(x,y)` reads the captured transmission coefficient. This fixes separate materials on one owner without reducing them to one owner-average color. Owner-level pigment/transmission remains the legacy-capture fallback; source operators that explicitly call `materialColor(id)` still intentionally use owner-level palettes. Arbitrary third-party shader hooks need an explicit pigment companion to expose custom procedural albedo.

Material boundaries now follow deterministic **16-bit profile tokens**, carried in the transmission pass's G/B channels; R retains the physical transmission coefficient. Profiles include linear pigment, material parameters, source/custom shader identity and stable traversal-based texture bindings. Equivalent native material slots share a token, while distinct pigments or shader profiles remain distinct even when their PBR scalars match. `ShaderMaterial` and `RawShaderMaterial` instances additionally receive stable traversal ordinals: different custom instances remain separate even when they share program text and differ only in uniforms; reuse of the same instance retains its token. This conservative distinction does not serialize arbitrary uniforms and does not expose custom procedural pigment without its explicit companion. Native PBR and all 22 Atelier source-factory profiles retain their existing grouping. The frame's `materialKey` and `sameDrawSurface` use those profiles; textured pixels do not become separate material regions. The profile table travels through the worker with the capture. A capture can represent up to 65,535 nonzero material profiles.

All **seven auxiliary pixel passes**—normal, linear depth, identity, diffuse, material properties, albedo and transmission—preserve eligible material slots individually. A visible, depth-writing slot with opacity above 0.2 is admitted even when another slot on the same mesh is an excluded glow. The passes retain each slot's front/back/double sidedness, map and alpha-map UV channels, alpha tests/hash, vertex alpha, native deformation and clipping behavior. Triangle snapshots use the same slot eligibility and sidedness policy. The actual GL diagnostic checked **85,536 foreground pixels across all seven masks with zero mismatches**, plus all **14 alpha/sidedness probes**, and restored the original scene/material state. Pixel alpha coverage is distinct from geometry topology: texture/shader holes still are not physical openings in the copied triangles.

World reconstruction supports perspective and orthographic projections. Conjure depth normalization uses its captured visible range; line/section interpolation uses reciprocal perspective depth. Geometry restoration supplies real samples, creases, section lines, surface voxels, curvature hatches, open boundaries and owner-unioned membranes. Original non-Conjure Garden paths retain their historical contracts.

Surface Pointillism retains the source budget of 36,000 world-area anchors over the whole subject geometry before visibility and density filtering. Large perspective interiors can therefore show sparse foreground marks and a denser distant cluster; this is a density/legibility limit, not an offscreen-clipping failure.

Shared surface samples retain finite projected coordinates and true geometric facing even when offscreen. Naturalist Plate reconstructs those hidden anchors in world space and keeps its sampled graph stable through camera orbit; point drawing applies its own viewport and lens-range clipping. This avoids collapsing offscreen anchors onto a screen-edge sentinel.

`engine/render-camera.ts` keeps one rendering camera per ordinary orthographic/perspective lens type, copying the exact world transform, projection/inverse, view offset, viewport and reversed-depth state. The authored world camera keeps its own ownership and animation/temporary transforms. Stable rendering-camera IDs prevent Three from accumulating a new glass-transmission target on every scene mount. The repeated glass/linen/perlage cycles now remain at **24 geometries and seven textures**; this is the measured fixture result, not a universal scene resource limit.

## Geometry, optics and resource limits

See [Variable-scene geometry proxies](atelier-proxy-port.md) for full Section ribs / Single cutaway / Interior lattice construction and limits. They derive current triangles and rebuild their own depth and shadows; none reuses the fixed Garden geometry after removing its signature guard. Material groups with a common body ID rejoin for closed topology. Negative scales repair winding. Open/non-manifold/alpha-cutout bodies remain sheets where an interior cannot be justified. BCC point/segment clearance predicates are evaluated around each body center at unit body scale, so dimensional distance and dimensionless barycentric tolerances stay meaningful. Only predicates are normalized; bar vertices remain in their original world coordinates. Tests preserve the accepted bar graph and whole-prism containment at scales from 1e-6 to 1e9.

Shader-only openings and texture-alpha holes are not holes in the triangle snapshot. Proxy triangles can therefore cover alpha-cutout areas; proxies omit native PBR reflection/transmission/procedural-relief optics as the source matte mechanisms do. Partial/object-scoped layers preserve original-image masks and may retain lower solid imagery. These are stated depiction limits, not silently unported study IDs.

Wholetoning retains its original objective, region ownership and connected-block optimizer; work is explicitly admitted by measured adjacency visits, and a depleted budget stops before an incomplete block. It does not swallow an exception or substitute a threshold filter. The original two-color result must not fail a “minimum three colors” image check. Directional color retains full 720px, three-pass computation and source-byte cache ownership. Continuous thread and Quilled paper do not require original scene triangles; the latter constructs and shades its own real paper relief.

The three existing composition finishes (finite lenses, completed-mix ink, fluted glass) remain separately loaded through the original image finisher. The bridge disposes each finish worker after completion/error. All three were exercised on a composed geometry mix, with deterministic replay and unchanged source capture.
